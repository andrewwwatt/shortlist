🔖 The Short List — Week of 18–21 August 2026

Food inflation hit a 16-year low, the rand jumped on a surprise US Treasury bond buyback, and South Africa's rugby fans are counting down to the biggest Test series in a generation. Meanwhile, 1,200 ducks are doing pest control at a Stellenbosch winery — and honestly, that's the energy we're bringing this week.

1. 🦆 Ducks > robots: a Stellenbosch winery's 1,200-strong eco-army

Business Day · Gugu Lourie · 20 August 2026

While China is busy programming humanoid robots to replace workers, Vergenoegd Löw Wine Estate has a far more elegant solution. Twice a day, 1,200 Indian Runner ducks march in formation through the vineyards, devouring snails and insects while naturally fertilising the soil. No pesticides, no robots — just 330 years of heritage, solar power, and a duck parade. The estate is a WWF Conservation Champion and one of the world's Top 100 Sustainable Hotels. Satisfied, unbothered, and perfectly in balance.

4. 🏉 Springboks vs All Blacks: the greatest rivalry starts Saturday

The Rugby's Greatest Rivalry series kicks off at Ellis Park this Saturday at 17:10. It's the first of four Tests — including one in Baltimore, USA — and New Zealand's first full-scale tour of South Africa in 30 years. The Boks are ranked #1, the All Blacks #2. If you're anywhere near a TV this weekend, you know where to be.

Formula 1 returns from its mid-season break to Zandvoort for the final-ever Dutch Grand Prix. It's a Sprint weekend, so racing on all three days. 6. 📱 SA TikTok creators call for 1 September boycott

Explain · Calay Blaauw · 18 August 2026

South Africa has 17 million TikTok users, but the platform's Creator Rewards Programme stops at the continent's border. A growing campaign is calling for a boycott on 1 September, arguing that SA creators drive trends and culture but get zero direct payment from TikTok. Even Communications Minister Solly Malatsi has weighed in, calling it "economic justice." The question: if a view in Johannesburg is worth the same as a view in London to advertisers, why isn't it worth the same to TikTok?

South Africa's food inflation has fallen to its lowest level in 16 years. After years of painful grocery bills, the numbers are finally moving in the right direction — giving the Reserve Bank room to hold rates and households a little more breathing room.
