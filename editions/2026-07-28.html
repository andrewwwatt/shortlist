🔖 Short List — Trial Run (Week of 22–28 July 2026)

The SA news backdrop this week: Treasury finally released R7.1bn to municipalities, SA-Nigeria talks cooled migrant-protest tensions, Angola's kwanza joined the SADC payment network, and the Madlanga Commission / IDAC corruption probe spectacularly collapsed. Good vibes: fuel prices dropped sharply on 1 July and the World Cup buzz is still in the air.

1. 🏡 Joburg's grand property revival

Currency News · Joan Muller · 27 July

Big-ticket home sales are back in Joburg as wealthy buyers rediscover value in the city's top suburbs. While Cape Town gets all the headlines, the City of Gold is quietly staging a comeback in the luxury segment.

2. 🍸 Cape Town's wealth boom draws a private members' club

Moneyweb · Prinesha Naidoo (Bloomberg) · 28 July

The flip side: Cape Town's surging wealth and influence has attracted an international private members' club. A nice companion to the Joburg story — SA's two poles are both drawing serious money, just in different ways.

3. 📉 When marketing misses the market

The Starbucks disaster in South Korea cost a CEO his job, a brand its market, and thousands of customers their loyalty. Olivier walks through several corporate marketing catastrophes that should never have happened. A great "did you hear about…" dinner-party story.

4. 💊 Aspen's fat profits from thinner people

Ghost Mail · The Finance Ghost · 21 July

One of the best headlines of the year. The Ghost unpacks how Aspen is riding the global weight-loss drug wave — and what it means for one of SA's pharma stalwarts on the JSE.

5. ⚡ Affordable EVs gain traction as petrol costs bite

Moneyweb · Jeremy Maggs · 28 July

With fuel prices heading up again after the July reprieve, affordable electric vehicles are starting to make genuine economic sense for South African households. A practical, forward-looking consumer story.

Explain · Staff Reporter · 28 July

We've all asked this. Explain breaks down where those disappearing megabytes go, what ICASA says, and why the system works the way it does. Accessible consumer journalism at its best.

Lab-grown diamonds have upended everything. This smart, elegantly written piece asks whether one of history's greatest marketing machines can revive the allure of mined stones. Business meets glamour, perfectly pitched.

Explain · Staff Reporter · 27 July

Desiree Ellis' side begins their Women's Africa Cup of Nations campaign. A talented squad, momentum from the World Cup, and a genuinely uplifting SA sports story to counterbalance all the hard news.
