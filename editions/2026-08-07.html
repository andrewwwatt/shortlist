Ghost Mail Weekender – Dominique Olivier

Japan is no matcha for Chinese disruption

The global matcha market is worth $5.1bn and forecast to nearly double to $8.9bn by 2033 – a boom that has quietly outgrown Japan's ability to supply it. Meanwhile, China's Guizhou province blew past its own 2025 forecast of 5,000 tonnes to produce 12,000 – roughly 70% of global output. It's a small, oddly specific example of a much bigger pattern: as Evan Osnos argues in a New Yorker piece this week, there's scarcely a market – biotech, EVs, batteries, now apparently tea – that China can't decide to take on and simply out-produce everyone else into second place. An unmatched knack for it, really.

A creepy problem

There is a name for the reason your savings never seem to catch up with your salary: lifestyle creep. Every raise quietly funds a slightly nicer life – a better car, more takeout, a slightly bigger house – until the gap between what you earn and what you actually build widens without anyone deciding it should. Is there a cure for this particular kind of creep?

What’s On This Weekend

Local Dispatches – Women's Day Weekend

Gauteng – A rare opportunity to enjoy the CBD

If you were one of the thousands who turned out for July's launch of Main Street Sundays, the sequel is here – Johannesburg's car-free Sunday series takes over Main Street in Marshalltown on 9 August with markets, live music and local traders. Free entry, register at jozimyjozi.com.

Gauteng – Hot-air balloons over the Cradle of Humankind

For Gauteng's other Women's Day option: two days of hot-air balloons over UNESCO World Heritage terrain at WildWood, paired with sparkling wine, live music and food stalls. Runs 8–9 August.

Western Cape – History, but make it a party

Izi-Women 2026 takes over three of Cape Town's Iziko heritage sites on 8 August to mark the 1956 march on the Union Buildings – think a free Company's Garden wellness session, a Long Table Dialogue with real teeth, and a musical send-off at the Old Town House. Closer to a festival than a commemoration.

KZN – Lira, Nomfundo Moh and DJ Sbu headline Durban's Women's Day concert

Old Mutual's Music at the Lake series returns to Durban Botanic Gardens on 9 August, with proceeds going toward the Gardens' Garden Window Project. Bring a picnic blanket – gates open early afternoon.

Everyone’s Talking About

Everyone's Talking About

Loo Shoes. I Sh*t you not.

You know fibre is having a moment when a company called Let Loose (we could not make this sh*t up if we tried) designs a pair of shoes specifically to help create a better squatting position and "improve bowel movement" – and it goes viral. They're called SH*T Shoes, and in a truth-is-stranger-than-fiction turn, the completely ridiculous shoes actually signpost one of the year's biggest health trends: "fibermaxxing," with health outlets reporting page views on fibre content up by orders of magnitude and Gen Z driving demand for fibre-rich foods across TikTok.

The real headline hiding under the puns: most of us eat roughly half the recommended daily fibre, and the gut-health payoff is genuine – even if, mercifully, the shoes remain optional.
